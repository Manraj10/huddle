# Submission checklist

## Before 8:00 AM

- Open the Google Form and screenshot every question before you need it
- Confirm whether a video, team IDs, repo links, or other attachments are required
- Make sure the repo is public and stays public through and after the event
- Save the final public URL and the room URL in one place
- Confirm the exact track and track justification before opening the form

## MLH conversation

Ask the rep, in these words:

1. Which MLH categories are live at HackCMU 2026?
2. Submission here is a Google Form with no MLH field — where do we opt in?
3. Is a demo video required for MLH categories, and how long?
4. Our repo is public and everything was built during the event — is anything else needed?
5. Is the Cursor prize announced at opening real? It is not on the site.

## Demo requirements

- Keep 2–3 spare phones already joined and face-down on the table
- Never demo with fewer than four phones
- Use the room screen to show the truth the players cannot see
- Have a backup paper QR code in case the laptop or Wi-Fi drops
- Start with Flash for the clock-sync moment, then Blindside for the hidden-information laugh, then the seat placement reveal

## Backup video

Record a 60-second phone video at 2:00 PM with:

- four phones on a table
- one round of Blindside
- one round of Flash
- the room screen visible for the whole shot

This is the fallback if the network fails during judging or if the room screen needs to carry the demo.

## Final submission

Submit at 3:30 PM, not 3:59. Keep the repo public after the event and note that the project was built during the hackathon.

## Track justification

Multiplayer is the right track because Huddle requires physical co-presence. It does not work as a single-person project; it only works when people share the same room, look at each other, and react in real time.
